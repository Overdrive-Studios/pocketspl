Getting started

Slide the switch on. After the start-up screen the big number is the sound level in dB, and the bar under it moves with the level:

ComfortableGetting loudToo loud

Where amber and red start is up to you (phone: Settings → General).

Top leftWeighting: dB(A), dB(C) or dB(Z)
Top middleWiFi. Blue when your phone can reach the meter
Top rightBattery
BottomMAX (or time, in LEQ) and the response

Charge with any USB-C charger.

The button

Hold 1 s, let goFAST → SLOW → LEQ → RTA → GIG
Double-click, then tapWeighting A → C → Z
Hold 3 sWiFi on / off

A single tap on its own does nothing, so a knock in a bag can't change anything. Choosing LEQ restarts LEQ and MAX.

The screens

  • FAST / SLOW: the level right now. FAST follows every hit, SLOW is steadier.
  • LEQ: the average level since you chose it, with how long it's been running.
  • RTA: 31 bars, low notes on the left to high on the right.
  • GIG: the average over the last 15 minutes (or your limit's time), against your limit, plus the day's dose.

Your phone

  1. Make sure WiFi is on (hold the button 3 s if the WiFi symbol isn't showing).
  2. On your phone, join the WiFi network PocketSPL, password pocketspl.
  3. Open 192.168.4.1 in Safari or Chrome.

You'll see the live level, a 60-second graph, the RTA, the gig log and every setting.

Better: use your own WiFi

On the phone page, Settings → WiFi, add your home, studio or venue WiFi. The meter joins it at switch-on and shows its address on screen (for example 192.168.1.47). On an iPhone you can also open pocketspl.local. If none of your saved networks is around, it makes its own network again.

Tip: in Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen makes the page open like an app.

Calibration

To check or redo the calibration you need a 94 dB / 1 kHz sound calibrator.

  1. Phone: Settings → General → Calibration → Calibrate with 94 dB calibrator.
  2. Fit the calibrator over the mic holes, switch it on, tap OK.
  3. Hold it still for 7 seconds. The meter saves the result itself.

You can also nudge it by hand with the −1 / −.1 / +.1 / +1 buttons against a meter you trust. Back to factory calibration undoes any change.

Software updates

  1. Open the phone page while your phone has internet (mobile data, or the meter on your own WiFi).
  2. Settings → Software. If there's a new version, tap Install update.
  3. Keep the page open. The meter installs it and restarts in about 30 seconds.

Updates keep your settings, calibration, saved networks and saved gigs. Once the phone has downloaded an update it can install it later without internet.

Troubleshooting

Forgot the meter's WiFi name or password

Switch off. Hold the button, switch on, and keep holding for 5 seconds. The WiFi goes back to PocketSPL / pocketspl.

The phone page says "Not connected"

Check the WiFi symbol on the meter. Grey means your phone has left the meter's network. Phones do this when a network has no internet. Rejoin it, and turn mobile data off while you use the meter, or put the meter on your own WiFi.

The screen says "Mic fault"

The microphone isn't sending anything. Switch off and on again. If it stays, contact us.

CLIP

It's louder than the mic can measure (around 120 dB). The reading is too low while CLIP shows.

Factory reset

Phone: Settings → General → Factory reset. Puts every setting back as new and deletes saved gigs. The factory calibration is kept.

USB repair

If the meter won't start properly or an update was interrupted, you can reload its software from a computer.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a Mac or Windows computer.
  2. Plug the meter in with a USB-C data cable and switch it on.
  3. Press the button for your meter below, pick the port that appears, and follow the steps.
This browser can't do USB repair. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. USB repair needs the page opened over https.

Not sure which board you have? Contact us with the serial number from Settings → General. If it asks to erase the meter, choose not to, so your settings and calibration are kept.
